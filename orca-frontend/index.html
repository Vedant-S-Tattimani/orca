PAGE 01
01 — Overview

Discover ORCA

What is ORCA?

ORCA — Marine Intelligence Platform is designed to make complex ocean and marine information easier to understand and use. Marine data often comes from different sources and can be difficult to interpret. ORCA brings important information together into one intelligent platform.

Using interactive maps, data visualizations, analytical tools, and AI-powered assistance, ORCA helps users explore marine conditions and transform raw information into meaningful insights.

Related Questions
Q: What problem does ORCA solve?
ORCA reduces the difficulty of accessing and understanding scattered and complex marine information.
Q: What makes ORCA different?
Instead of presenting users with raw datasets alone, ORCA focuses on making marine information easier to explore, understand, and use for better decision-making.
PAGE 02
02 — Data Architecture

Data & Intelligence

What information does ORCA work with?

ORCA brings together different types of marine and environmental information to create a clearer picture of ocean conditions. Depending on the available data sources, users can explore information related to ocean surface conditions, weather, waves, environmental indicators, and geospatial marine observations.

The platform presents this information through visual dashboards and interactive maps, helping users understand relationships and patterns that may be difficult to identify from raw data.

Related Questions
Q: Where does the information come from?
ORCA is designed to work with trusted marine, environmental, satellite, and weather data sources.
Q: Is the information easy to understand?
Yes. One of ORCA's main goals is to transform complex information into clearer visualizations and understandable insights.
PAGE 03
03 — Yield Analytics

Fishing Intelligence

How can ORCA support fishing decisions?

Fishing decisions can depend on changing marine and environmental conditions. ORCA's Fishing Intelligence feature helps users explore relevant ocean indicators and identify areas that may be potentially suitable for fishing.

Instead of relying on a single piece of information, the platform brings multiple marine conditions into the decision-making process and presents them visually on an interactive map.

Related Questions
Q: Does ORCA guarantee a catch?
No. ORCA provides information and insights to support decisions. Natural marine conditions and fishing outcomes can always vary.
Q: Who can benefit from this feature?
Fishermen and marine stakeholders can use these insights to better understand surrounding conditions before planning activities at sea.
PAGE 04
04 — Safety & Navigation

Maps, Routes & Safety

How does ORCA help users navigate marine conditions?

ORCA provides interactive marine maps that help users visualize important ocean and environmental information geographically. Instead of reading large amounts of data, users can explore conditions directly on a map.

The Route Planning and Safety features can help users consider marine conditions when planning journeys and understanding potentially challenging situations at sea.

Related Questions
Q: What can I explore on the map?
Users can explore available marine layers, environmental information, ocean conditions, and other geospatial insights.
Q: Is ORCA a replacement for official safety systems?
No. ORCA is an information and decision-support platform. Users should always follow official warnings, regulations, and maritime safety guidance.
PAGE 05
05 — Conversational AI

The ORCA AI Assistant

How does the AI Assistant work?

Marine information can be technical and difficult to search. The ORCA AI Assistant allows users to interact with the platform using natural language. Instead of navigating multiple datasets and technical interfaces, users can simply ask questions.

The assistant is designed to help explain available information, summarize conditions, and guide users toward useful insights in a more conversational way.

Examples of questions you can ask:
  • "What are the current marine conditions in this area?"
  • "Which factors should I consider before planning a route?"
  • "Explain these ocean conditions in simple terms."
  • "What information is available for this region?"

The goal is simple: make complex marine information more accessible to everyone.

06 — Vision & Mission

One Platform, Many Insights

Why ORCA?

The ocean generates an enormous amount of valuable information, but that information is often spread across different systems and difficult for everyday users to interpret.

ORCA brings together marine intelligence, interactive mapping, fishing insights, route planning, safety monitoring, AI assistance, and analytical reports within a unified experience.

Our Mission

Transform complex marine information into accessible intelligence that helps people better understand the ocean and make more informed decisions.

ORCA is built around one central idea:

"The ocean is full of data. ORCA helps turn it into understanding."
